How it works

EZTiger puts a song list of your library online, with its own web address and QR code. Only titles and artists go online; your song files never leave your laptop.

Singer scansyour QR code on their phone (own data, any network)
Picks a songsearches your songs, adds their name, presses Send
You accept iton your DJ list: ✓ Add to queue, or ✕ Reject
It's queuedin EZTiger's Tonight's playlist, with the singer's name

What you need

  • EZTiger Pro on Windows, build BUILD or later. Older builds don't have the Get my online request page button.
  • Your songs in EZTiger: your karaoke folders added and scanned.
  • Internet on your laptop at the venue: the venue Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot. It uses very little data.
Hidden folders stay hidden. Only folders in your searchable library go on the song list. To keep a folder (backing tracks, say) off it but still playable, right-click the folder → Keep out of the songbook.

Set it up (once)

Do this at home, with the laptop on the internet.

Name your show

Go to ☰ Menu → Songbook and type the Heading singers will see (for example "Dave's Karaoke Nights"), then Close.

Get your own request page

Go to ☰ Menu → Song requests and press 🌐 Get my online request page. In a few seconds the box fills with your DJ link and EZTiger says ✓ Connected.

Save your DJ link

Press Copy the DJ link and paste it somewhere safe, such as an email to yourself. It opens your DJ list on any device and reconnects EZTiger if you reinstall. Don't share it: anyone with it can run your list.

Put your songs on it

Press Close, then ☰ Menu → Start song requests. EZTiger sends your song list (a big library takes a minute), opens your DJ list in your web browser, and points your songbook QR code at your new page.

Try it with your phone

Open ☰ Menu → Songbook → Show QR code. Scan it with your phone on mobile data and send yourself a request. It pops up under New requests on the DJ list.

On the night

Open EZTiger, connect the laptop to the internet, and press ☰ Menu → Start song requests. Your DJ list opens in the browser.

"⚠ Not found in EZTiger" beside a queued request means that song isn't in the library on this laptop (a different laptop, or a renamed file). Find it and add it to the playlist by hand.

Give your DJ list its own taskbar button

With the DJ list open in Google Chrome:

  • Click the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar (or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app), then Install.
  • It opens in its own window with the EZTiger tiger. Right-click the tiger on the taskbar → Pin to taskbar.

You can open the same DJ list on a tablet or phone too: open your DJ link there. Keep EZTiger open on the laptop so accepted songs go into Tonight's playlist.

No signal at the venue?

Online requests need the internet on your laptop. If there's no venue Wi-Fi and your phone has no signal, use your own Wi-Fi router instead.

Switch to this PC

Go to ☰ Menu → Song requests and press Use this PC instead (no internet). Connect the laptop to your router, then ☰ Menu → Start song requests.

Singers join your router's Wi-Fi

They turn mobile data off and scan the QR code, which now points at your laptop. The first time, if Windows asks about PowerShell, tick both Private and Public and press Allow.

Back online next time

In ☰ Menu → Song requests press Use the online request list. Your online address and DJ link haven't changed.

If something's not right

Phones say "The song list isn't up yet"Press ☰ Menu → Start song requests to send your songs.
The DJ list says "EZTiger not connected"Open EZTiger on the laptop. ☰ Menu → Song requests should say ✓ Connected.
"Too many new request pages today"Each connection can make a few pages a day. You only need one: if you already have a DJ link, paste it into the box and press Connect.
New laptop or EZTiger reinstalledDon't make a new page. Paste your saved DJ link into ☰ Menu → Song requests, press Connect, then Start song requests. Your QR code and posters still work.
Lost your DJ linkOn the laptop: ☰ Menu → Song requests → Copy the DJ link.
